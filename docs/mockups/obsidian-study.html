Obsidian 黑曜

Black panels that glow at the edges, and a card tray under the figure where cards slide in. Three directions for the panels; the tray behaves the same in each. Gold still only marks what you can act on; the rim light is the crack light.

Motion
A · Still rimThe edge catches light from above, brightest at the corners. Calm, cheapest.
B · Living edgeA slow light walks the border; it quickens and brightens while a card waits.
C · Qi-litThe dantian lights the panel from inside, and the rim breathes with the qi bar.
A · Still rim

Static gradient border plus a soft outer glow. Nothing moves but the lacquer sheen, which drifts across once every fourteen seconds. Safe for hours on screen.

B · Living edge

An animated conic gradient on the border. Doubles as the "something waits" signal: the tray's light runs faster when a card is pending, so no badge is needed.

C · Qi-lit

The most tied to the game: the panel is lit by your own qi. A full bar makes the abode bright; an empty one leaves it dim. Pairs well with B's waiting signal on the tray only.
