# Place screens: a brief for screen inspo

Paste this to ChatGPT to make layout inspo images for the place screens. It describes the layouts we settled; ask for one image per screen in the list at the end.

## The game

Severed Heaven 断天 is a xianxia cultivation game on PC monitors. Each screen is a 1920×1080 frame: a narrow icon rail on the far left (72 px, dark, gold icons), no bar across the top, the game's name floating at the top left, and the season, the hour, the spirit stones and the insight floating at the top right.

**The look:** obsidian and gilt. A black void, dark see-through obsidian panels edged by a single thin gold hairline with a small corner radius, thin elegant type (a serif for names, a clean sans for text), names in English with their hanzi in brush script beside them in gold. Lots of dark, and the richness comes from one big painted scene per screen. Sleek and quiet, never cluttered, never neon.

**The world:** Chinese cultivation-novel xianxia at continental scale. Cities are 150 li across, with walls as tall as cliffs raised by formations; sects stand on peaks with halls, pavilions and stairs; arrays show as domes of light, and beacon fire as pillars. Never medieval Europe, never sci-fi. These places are in the Border Wilds, a beast-haunted frontier: war cities, coasts, fens, karst.

**The paintings:** every place has its own painting. Two cities never share one, so they shouldn't look alike.

## Entering a place

On the travel map you enter a place, and its screen replaces the map. Every place screen keeps the same head, over the left of its banner: a "← Map" button and breadcrumb (The Border Wilds › City of the Guarded Mouth), a square seal with one hanzi, the name with its hanzi, the pinyin, one line of facts (thriving city · coast · 150 li · top realm Nascent Soul), and two or three lines of lore.

**The banner stays on top.** When you open a door, only the part below the banner changes to that door's own layout.

Within a job, the controls stay the same: every shop has the same item rows (a small thumbnail, the name in its rarity color, a line under it, the price in gold, and a Buy button), however differently its room is dressed.

## Layouts by kind of place

1. **City and town** (City of the Guarded Mouth 守口城, a coastal war city ringed by walls 150 li across; Pacified Fort 安边堡, a war city cut into a flattened hill crown with cliff-tall walls and a beacon of spirit-fire). A wide painted banner across the top with the head over its left. Under it, a slim gate strip ("The watch at the Sea Gate reads Qi Condensation 6, and lets you in"). Under that, group tabs (Markets · Hear, hire and rest · Work), a 3×3 grid of painted door cards (each a small painted scene with a seal, a name and one line), and to the right of the grid a preview of the picked door (the city screen is a navigation hub, not the shop): its name, how many shops, a flavor line, tags for what it sells, its notable shops with their keepers, one market note, and two buttons at the bottom, Go in (walk the street as a story scene) and Enter (open its shop room). The city's ruling power (the Myriad Crafts Guild) is one door that opens its hall.
2. **Hall: any sect, clan, guild or temple** (the Lamp-Keeper Sect 守灯宗, which keeps ten thousand lamps of yin fire on poles across the Ghost-Lamp Fens). A tall banner with a round crest, the name, a motto in spaced capitals and a few chips on the left, and a small panel at the top right with your standing (an outsider, rank, contribution). Under it, a row of five painted hall tiles, one of them **Inside 内**, and under the tiles the open hall full width: its picture on the left with tall vertical hanzi, its content in the middle, and a short note on the right. **Inside** opens the city layout (the 3×3 grid) for the sect's own halls: the exchange hall, the elders' hall, the training ground, the Mission hall, the scripture pavilion and the law hall.
3. **Gate: a sect behind an array** (Beast-Butcher Gate 屠兽门, a hard frontier sect that ranks its disciples by beast cores taken). The hall layout, but a dark red panel with a red seal stands where the tiles go: "The gate is shut to you. Its array is Core Formation", with greyed Show a token and Present an invitation buttons, and the hall tiles dimmed below it.
4. **Village** (Spear-Plow Village 枪犁村, where fields are plowed with spears at hand). Almost all painting, with the head at the top left over a dark fade, a row of five painted door cards along the bottom (the county store, the village elder, fields, a farmhouse to let, a bed in a barn), and the open door as a panel floating over the painting on the right.
5. **Wild site** (Blood-Vine Gorge 血藤峡, a gorge where a sect's slaughter yards drain and the vine grows blood-red to the root). The whole screen is the painting. A dark column on the left holds the head, a few facts (depth, beasts, finds) and the features (red vine on the walls; the runoff from the yards; a narrow floor), and a strip of painted action cards runs along the bottom (Stay and cultivate here, Pin it, Claim it, Your dwelling, Back to the map).

## Rooms: a layout per kind of door

The place's banner stays on top, and the bottom part becomes the room. A room's big picture is its door's painted scene, shown large.

- **Market** (the free market, the beast-part market, the grain and cloth market): a street of vendors as small cards, each opening its own counter.
- **Craft shop** (Pill Street, Refiners' Row, Talisman Lane): the keeper and their work in a big panel with the item rows beside it, dressed by its craft: a pill shop shows the day's batch from the furnace, a refiner has racks and a Mend panel, and a talisman shop has a wall of hanging talismans.
- **Trading hall** (the Qian Clan's trading hall, a sect's exchange hall): a ledger of lots grouped by where they came from, with the house's terms.
- **Board** (the bounty office, a sect's Mission hall, posts, commissions): postings in a column, each with an icon, a title, a line, the pay on the right and a Take it button.
- **Teahouse** (the most important one): the patrons sitting there today in a column on the left with portraits, the scene in the middle (one patron talking, a quote, choices under it), and actions on the right (Order tea, Hear a rumor, Rest upstairs).
- **Departures** (caravans, guides, the map seller, the broker): a board of rows with a painted band on each: route, destination, days, fee, and Depart or Hire.
- **Hall room** (the elders' hall, the training ground, the examination hall, the scripture pavilion, the law hall): its picture with vertical hanzi on the left, the content, and a note.
- **Changer**: a small exchange panel, stones in and silver out.
