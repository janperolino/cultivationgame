The Quiet Moon stands over the wharf on stilts, and the river's stink comes up through its floorboards with the steam. Bargemen crowd the long benches under the dais, where an old storyteller is killing the intendant's third concubine for the second time tonight. Auntie Wen pours without looking. Nobody here has a root worth testing except the man in grey by the far pillar, and every bargeman has found a reason not to sit near him.
