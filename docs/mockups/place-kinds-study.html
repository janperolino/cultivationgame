Places by kind

A6 study, block 1: one layout for each kind of place you walk into. The kinds are the code's (city, town, village, hall, gate, site, PlaceKindId), and every name, door, shop and line of lore is the Border Wilds' data. Within a job the controls stay the same: every counter has the same item rows, price and Buy, every door shows its seal, name and one line, and every place opens with the same head (the ← Map crumb, seal, name, hanzi, pinyin, the line, the lore). The pictures are stand-ins cropped from the inspo; each place paints its own. Blue dashed labels are notes, not UI.
