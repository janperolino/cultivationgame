Prepared ground 伏

A disc or flags set anywhere off a site: right-click a spot, Lay an array here. It needs no slot and lasts while its stones last.

A passive trap

Each day the land may send something across it: a beast of the land's band where it stands, about one day in ten (the world's roll, keyed by the spot and the day). One at its band or under is held until you come; a stronger one breaks through and the disc's stones are spent.

Holding burns five times the stones, so a beast left long enough eats the disc dry and walks off.

When you check

Its seal turns red when something waits. A held beast is fought bound, on the disc's ground, through the fights you play.
