Ways other games reveal the map

  • Climb to see (Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom): a region's map stays covered until you climb its tower and scan from up high. For us: a peak or a pagoda turns its kingdom Sketched.
  • Clear the camps (Ghost of Tsushima): camps clear the fog round them, and freeing every camp in a region clears the whole region. For us: a kingdom turns Seen once you've stood in all its towns.
  • Ask the locals (Where Winds Meet): the map stays greyed even where you've walked until you talk to a Wayfinder, a few per region. For us: the guide in a town, who already exists, sketches the kingdom.
  • Buy it, then rest to draw it in (Hollow Knight): the cartographer's map is partial, and new rooms only go onto it when you sit at a bench. For us: what you passed only goes onto the map when you stop at a town or camp.
  • Rumors as question marks (Outer Wilds ship log, player reports): leads you haven't followed show as grey cards with a question mark. For us: a rumor's mark as a 闻 seal or a "?" instead of a diamond.
  • Clouds that clear as you go (Civilization V, Valheim): the cover lifts in a circle round you as you travel. That's what we already do.

Sources: Civ V, Civ VI, Shogun 2, 0 A.D., Endless Legend, Northgard, Hollow Knight, Elden Ring, Tears of the Kingdom, Ghost of Tsushima, Where Winds Meet, Outer Wilds. These are each game's idea painted onto our map, not copies of their art. Where a source didn't describe a game's look, it isn't here.
