The bag, in squares

A throwaway mockup, outside the project. The grid is the capacity: pills and herbs stack to 99 a square, ore to 20, gear takes one and a furnace takes four. Gear hangs on the cultivator, stones are a count, and what you leave at home sits in a second grid. Click a thing to read it; drag it onto a gear square to wear it.
