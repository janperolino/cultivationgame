When a raid comes (proposed). Once a month, the world asks about each site you hold in the wild. Thieves come when they find it and it is worth more than its guard and more than their month's wage. Finding is the searcher's soul band against your concealment, with no dice. Their band is the land's, edge plus ring. Beasts come when they find it and something draws them: a density raised above the land, or a bed of ten years or more, which brings a beast a band stronger at a hundred years. Who comes and on which day is world content, seeded by the site and the month. A dwelling in a town or city is the watch's, like K8's plots: a thief only from heat 3.
