How the furnace reads

What you throw carries one element and one or two properties each. The furnace makes what most of it carries:

  • nourishinga qi pill
  • temperinga body pill
  • soul-calminga soul pill
  • restoringqi back in a fight
  • mendingwounds closed
  • purginga poison drawn out
  • toxicstronger, and more poison
