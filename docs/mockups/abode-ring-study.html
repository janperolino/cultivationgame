The day ring

The twelve hours round the figure, 子 at the top, each tinted by what it goes to. A light walks it with the current hour, and the motes and orbs come in from that point, so the ring is the source of the flow rather than a dial. It also says why the motes change color.

The mat

Your own things, set out in front of you. They come to any abode. Each is a data entry with a glyph; cards and orbs can touch them later.

The abode as numbers

The vein's grade sets how thick and bright the mote field is, its element biases the tint, and the spirit pool glows under the figure. At Foundation a floor of qi gathers under the seat, as the realm vocabulary already says.

What to judge. Whether the ring reads as the hour without a label; whether it fights the circulation inside the body; whether the mat is enough at M1 (stones and a manual) or needs M2 and M4 first; whether the numbers layer alone leaves the space feeling empty.
