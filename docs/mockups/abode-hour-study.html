The hour without an object

The ring and the mat are gone and the space stays empty. Two ways the hour can show on what is already there: the figure casts a shadow that turns with the sun, and the light on the glass comes from where the sun is. Drag the hour or let the clock run. Hover to catch an orb.

The sundial

The figure is the gnomon. At dawn the shadow is long and falls away from the sun; at noon it is a pool under the legs; at dusk it is long the other way. At night there is no sun, so the only light on the floor is the dantian's own, brighter as the bar fills. Nothing is added to the space, and nothing moves on its own except the sun.

The light wash

A faint light on the glass from where the sun stands: warm from the horizon at dawn, pale from above at noon, orange at dusk, and the yin blue low along the floor at night. Weak on purpose: the hour should be felt before it is read. The motes arrive from everywhere as before, since this is light, not qi.

Both

They are the same sun, so they agree: when the wash comes from the right, the shadow falls left. Together they say the hour; the caption still says it in words.
