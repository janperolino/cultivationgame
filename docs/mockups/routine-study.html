Routine and the road day

The Abode becomes Cultivate: the seated figure, the orbs and the breakthroughs. Today's Cultivate becomes Routine: the day wheel, Practice and Study. Routine's wheel gets a Home / Road toggle, so you can set the road's twelve hours before you leave. On either wheel you can pick several hours and give them all one kind.

Try it: drag across the wheel, shift-click to take a run of hours, ctrl-click to add or drop one hour. The dashed bar holds the open choices.

What changes

  1. Names, outright. Screen ids become cultivate (the figure) and routine (the wheel); AbodeScreen → CultivateScreen, today's CultivateScreen → RoutineScreen, the tab type and links follow. The unlocks and the seen record carry the ids, so save v27 moves abode→cultivate and cultivate→routine in both, in that order. The cave abode in the world (finding an abode site, spirit veins) keeps its word; only the screen is renamed.
  2. Cultivate takes 修炼, the hanzi Routine had. Routine takes a new one, picked above.
  3. Routines fold away. One line under the wheel names the routine you keep; Change opens the list: the learned ones (or the ways of the road), your own, and saving this wheel. Closed again, it is one line.
  4. The road day is a toggle at the head of Routine's first tab. Road shows the road wheel, the four road routines you can follow, and your own, which you can follow, rename and forget. Editing a road routine you didn't make copies it into your own (My road 2), as the Map does now. In a group the road wheel is the group's: you see it but can't edit it.
  5. Several hours at once, on both wheels: drag across, shift-click for a run, ctrl-click to add or drop one. The editor names the hours you picked; a kind button sets them all. Locked hours are skipped, and the editor says so. Esc or a click on the centre clears the pick.
  6. The Map bar keeps its small wheel and the routine chips for a quick switch. Tapping an hour either opens Routine on the road wheel with that hour picked, or keeps the bar's own one-hour picker. Pick one above.
