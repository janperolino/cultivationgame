The breath
The brush holds one breath of qi ink. It thins as you draw, by time, not by distance: dawdle and it runs dry before the foot. The thinner the ink, the narrower the line you must stay on, so the foot is the hard part; hurry the open curves to bank ink for it. Stray off the line and that stretch is spoiled; stray far and the qi snaps. Lift and the breath breaks.
