What to look at

  • The land: each tract has its own tint and marks (karst spires, hill arcs, pines, grass, reeds, waves), scattered across the world so the texture holds at every zoom. Rivers run to the west coast, with a ferry 渡 wherever a road crosses one. Both are mock: the atlas has no rivers in the Border Wilds yet.
  • The trail lights the band you walked; a tract you have seen shows dimly round it, with its name.
  • Sites: about 160 in the region now, in twenty kinds: springs 泉, waterfalls 瀑, thunder peaks 峰, bandit camps 寨, hermits 庐, mines 矿, graves 冢, ancient trees 木, a stele 碑, hamlets and the rest. About one in five is hidden.
  • No buttons for the routine: the road wheel has a look-around hour (酉 on Walk by day), you also look around when you arrive, and the night is spent at an inn, a shelter you found, or a camp, by rule. Watch the wheel's line while you walk.
  • Right-click anything: a town (enter, teahouse rumors, the kingdom map, a guide, a caravan, the inn), a checkpoint (pay, go round, talk), a cave (seclude), a spring (sit and cultivate), the Gate (token, invitation, force, sneak), a deadly land (go in anyway, go to its edge), a rumor (search, pin) or bare land (go, by night, pressed, seclude, pin). Grey lines arrive in the phase they name.

P3.1's data, read on the map

  • The City of the Guarded Mouth is tagged coast and has a reef road at low tide, but it sits about 16,000 li from the coast. The Night Bridge's sea caves are about 33,000 li inland. Either the points move to the west coast, or the coast moves.
  • Everything sits in the middle third of the region. The west and south are empty karst, and the tracts here are what would fill them.
  • The low-tide chain runs from the southwest coast to the Pushed Frontier. Beyond the region's edge is M6's long travel.
