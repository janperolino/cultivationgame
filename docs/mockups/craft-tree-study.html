Every craft tree is the same opening fan as the Daos. Tier 1 opens on a root seal at the hub (the starter recipe), then three branches, each two minors, a notable and its own capstone; a branch's capstone opens that branch's next band. Round nodes are perks, square nodes are recipe seals. Node names here are placeholders; the drafts come with the next message.

AThree rivers

Each branch is one straight spoke from the hub to the rim: Hand left, Line up the middle, Lore right. A pure Line alchemist climbs one river; the tier is held at 7 of its 13 nodes, so touching a second river pays. Fits the fan's code as it is (a branch is a spoke).

BThree wedges

Each branch owns a third of the band, faintly tinted: its two minors side by side, the notable behind them, the capstone at the wedge's rim. Reads more like three small trees in one; the Lore wedge's seals sit as a row of squares. Needs a wedge pass in the layout.
