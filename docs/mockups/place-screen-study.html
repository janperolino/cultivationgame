Entering a place

Right-click a place on the map and choose Enter, or arrive on a course marked to go in: the Map gives way to the place's own screen, with the map one step back. The gate comes first, then the place's doors on the left; a door opens at the counter. Try each kind of place, both counters, and how the watch reads your name.
