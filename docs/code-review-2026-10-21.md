**A. The tests that fail on the first run after a merge.** *(run)* No test depends on order or the clock. Nothing in a test reads time. The module caches in core are keyed by everything they read. Vitest isolates each file. The cause is load. About a dozen simulation-heavy tests take 2 to 4 s on a quiet machine against vitest's default limit of 5 s: `trees.test` "changes exactly the numbers each node claims" (3.8 s), `cards.test` "runs through catch-up with the day step" (3.6 s), `placing.test` (3.2 s), `played.test`, `seclusion.test`, `map.test`, `districts.test` ×2, `craftDeck.test`, `chains.test`, `loop.test`, `encounters.test` and `place.test`. Two full suites run at once time out 11 and 13 of them, and fail no assertion. A merge session's first run competes with whatever just ran (the merge, a build, another session's sims), which is why it is always the first run and why it passes on a rerun. `economy.test`'s 5.3 s test already sets its own 20 s.
- *Fix:* `testTimeout: 30_000` in `vite.config.ts`'s `test` block, so a slow machine is never a failure and a real hang still ends. Check it by running two suites at once again.

**B. `npm run sim -- stones` FAILs at Qi Condensation.** *(run)* Yes, a crafts change caused it: K5 (`a105f42`, talisman drawing). Bisected over main's merges with archived copies, it PASSes at every commit up to and including K4 (`e3b0649`) and K5's parent (`1153c79`), and FAILs from K5 on. K11 doesn't move it.
- The 14% is the mean, and it is inside the band (5% to 40%). What fails is the sim's other rule, that every market leaves something: Sinkhole Terraces goes from 3% left to −1% (0% today).
- K5's four new talismans (Golden Light, Body-Fixing, Earth Escape, Divine Travel) are all `talisman` kind, so they join the basket's talisman need. Earth Escape fills it at 10 low, and splitting the need over more fillers pressed Rockfall (3.27 → 5.66) and Pufferfish (4.4 → 7.62) at Sinkhole. That adds 0.8 low a month to the talisman line (3.35 → 4.14), against wages of 24.
- *Proposed fix:* a month's wear of talismans is strikes and wards, not passage. Earth Escape and Divine Travel are road tools. Have the basket's talisman need leave out the Passage family (`FILLED_BY.talisman` on a tag the strikes and wards carry, or a `basket: false` on the passage talismans), then rerun the sim. If Sinkhole still sits at 0%, the other lever is the talisman need's weight at tier 1 in `BASKET`. This touches the basket, not the crafts' pay.
