# Small calls

These are the small calls a phase made for later phases to revisit, moved here verbatim from `plan.md`'s Open questions. A phase reads the group for the systems it touches, in step 2 of How each session runs.

## The economy

- **Small calls in E1, for later phases to revisit.** A part-drunk stone pays a price posted in its own grade or above at what is left in it, but only a whole stone breaks, so a purse of 0.3 mid and nothing below can't pay a 1-low toll. An NPC's purse in a scene stays one count of low's worth, and only what crosses into yours is counted out by grade; splitting NPC purses waits for the living world. A card's or a road card's gain arrives in low (every written one is 1 to 20), a burglary's in its place's vein's grade, and an opened ring's in its tier's grade, since a sealed ring keeps no owner's realm.
- **Small calls in E2, for later phases to revisit.** A lent lot goes to any shop whose kind sells that kind of thing somewhere in the catalogs, so an alchemy shop never borrows a saber but a stall does; methods aren't lent (E3's). The bounty office pays 80% for a need too, as "any but the black market" reads. The City reaches Spear-Plow Village at 29,464 li, just inside 30,000, so its chandler carries Spear-Plow's millet cakes.
