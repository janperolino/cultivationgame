# Hatchling Barns 雏棚村, the art brief

A village, the seat of a farming county, shown in the village layout (A6). 2 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then one door sheet of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

- `place-hatchling-barns-banner.png`
- `place-hatchling-barns-doors-1.png`

Run them in one chat, the banner first, so the door sheets keep its look.

## 1. The banner: `place-hatchling-barns-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Hatchling Barns (Chupeng Cun 雏棚村), a village, the seat of a farming county, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Make it unmistakably Hatchling Barns, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game.

ITS LOOK: Long low barns sunk into a valley floor under a warmth array that hangs a faint amber haze, crates stacked at doors that open only after dark, lamps shuttered, ramps down to warm hatching pens, and carts that come in empty-looking at night. Colours: amber haze, barn timber brown, night blue, straw yellow. It feels secretive, warm, humid, uneasy. Leave out: crowds, markets, banners, grand halls, towers, daytime bustle.

From the world map it shows as: Long barns sunk into a valley under a warmth array that keeps the frost off them, a faint amber haze. Within it: the hatchling barns.

SCALE: it is about 6 li (3 km) across, home to about 60,000 mortals, 80 at Qi Condensation. Paint it at that true size from a nearby height: a settlement of thousands, its houses at human size and its few cultivators’ works far larger than life. Never a small medieval town.

The land: open grass plains to the horizon, wide sky, herds and wind.

The light: a bright overcast morning, soft light, colours clear. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows the whole painting behind a few cards: keep the top-left third calmer and darker (the name and lore sit there) and the bottom quarter calm near ground (a row of door cards sits there); put the place itself in the centre and right.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Hatchling Barns, Chupeng Cun 雏棚村, in the Border Wilds.

- The Night Bridge’s crates come in after dark, and nobody asks what hatches.

- It is about 6 li (3 km) across.

- Its people: about 60,000 mortals, 80 at Qi Condensation.

- Within it: the hatchling barns.
```
