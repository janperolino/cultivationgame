## 1. The banner: `place-pacified-fort-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Pacified Fort (Anbian Bao 安边堡), a town, the seat of a county, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Attached is Pacified Fort as it is painted on the game's world map, seen from very high: this is the same place up close, so keep its architecture, its colours and the way it sits on the land.

Make it unmistakably Pacified Fort, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game.

ITS LOOK: A war city cut into a flattened hill crown: walls of the hill’s own rock, barracks in long identical rows, drill grounds as vast bare plazas with soldiers in ranks, armouries and spear racks, black-and-red Gongsun banners, the March’s beacon burning on the highest bastion. Colours: dun stone, iron, black and red. It feels square, disciplined, austere. Leave out: gilt, ponds and gardens, bazaar clutter, the sea.

From the world map it shows as: A war city on a hill crown cut flat, ringed by walls of its own rock as tall as cliffs, its drill grounds pale scars across the plateau, a pale lockdown dome over it, and the March’s beacon as a pillar of spirit-fire on its highest bastion. Within it: the beacon bastion; the drill ground on the cut plateau; the armouries under the walls; the cadet school’s lecture halls.

SCALE: it is about 40 li (20 km) across, home to about 1,500,000 mortals, 1,500 at Qi Condensation, 120 at Foundation. Paint it at that true size from a nearby height: a great city in its own right that fills the whole view, streets as wide as rivers, its biggest halls the size of hills. Never a small medieval town.

The land: folded green hill country, terraced fields in the valleys, beacon peaks on the ridgelines.

The light: a moonlit night, silver light on stone and water, lamps warm against it. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Pacified Fort, Anbian Bao 安边堡, in the Border Wilds.

- The Gongsun war city and the March’s seat, cut into the crown of a hill the first general flattened with one stroke. Its walls are the hill’s own rock, raised by formation to the height of cliffs; inside them, barracks, armouries and drill grounds hold four hundred thousand under arms, and every sixteen-year-old the March owes the garrison passes through its cadet school. The March’s beacon burns on its highest bastion, and a lockdown array rises over it as a dome of pale light when beasts come.

- It is about 40 li (20 km) across.

- Its people: about 1,500,000 mortals, 1,500 at Qi Condensation, 120 at Foundation.

- Within it: the beacon bastion; the drill ground on the cut plateau; the armouries under the walls; the cadet school’s lecture halls.
```
