# Thornpost 刺栅, the art brief

A village, the seat of a farming county, shown in the village layout (A6). 2 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then one door sheet of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

- `place-thornpost-banner.png`
- `place-thornpost-doors-1.png`

Run them in one chat, the banner first, so the door sheets keep its look.

## 1. The banner: `place-thornpost-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Thornpost (Cizha 刺栅), a village, the seat of a farming county, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Make it unmistakably Thornpost, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game.

ITS LOOK: A hunters’ post ringed by a dark thorn hedge as tall as a tower, forty li round, grown by a wood-root cultivator: the outer rind a wall of black thorn with a narrow gap-gate, huts of fen timber and hide inside, drying racks of game, and the fens beyond fading into mist. Colours: thorn black-green, mist grey, hide brown, damp straw. It feels wary, defensive, rough, edge of the wild. Leave out: stone walls, towers, bazaars, ornament, open water, banners.

From the world map it shows as: A ring of dark thorn hedge forty li round, plain against the fen. Within it: the thorn hedge.

SCALE: it is about 4 li (2 km) across, home to about 15,000 mortals, 20 at Qi Condensation. Paint it at that true size from a nearby height: a settlement of thousands, its houses at human size and its few cultivators’ works far larger than life. Never a small medieval town.

The land: karst country: stone pillars by the hundred standing out of a sea of cloud, sinkholes and caves.

The light: a storm rolling in, rain curtains and lightning far off. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows the whole painting behind a few cards: keep the top-left third calmer and darker (the name and lore sit there) and the bottom quarter calm near ground (a row of door cards sits there); put the place itself in the centre and right.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Thornpost, Cizha 刺栅, in the Border Wilds.

- A hunters’ post on the way to the fens, ringed by a thorn hedge as tall as a tower that a wood-root cultivator grew a century ago against what comes out of them.

- It is about 4 li (2 km) across.

- Its people: about 15,000 mortals, 20 at Qi Condensation.

- Within it: the thorn hedge.
```
