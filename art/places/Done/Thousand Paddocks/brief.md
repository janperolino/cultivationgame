# Thousand Paddocks 千栏城, the art brief

A city, shown in the grid layout (A6). 3 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then 2 door sheets of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

## 1. The banner: `place-qianlan-cheng-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Thousand Paddocks (Qianlan Cheng 千栏城), a city, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Make it unmistakably Thousand Paddocks, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game.

ITS LOOK: The herders’ royal city of three million inside rings of array-fenced paddocks sixty li across: the king’s riding court as an open ring of sand under awnings, felted-tent and timber halls of the great herder families, the remount officers’ long hall, herds moving between rings like cloud shadow, and riders everywhere on thunder-horned deer and scale-horses. Colours: grass green, horse and hide brown, felt white, saddle red. It feels open, equestrian, boisterous, sprawling. Leave out: stone walls, skulls, terraces, ships, narrow lanes, towers.

From the world map it shows as: Pale rings of array-fenced paddock in rings round a royal city sixty li across, the herds moving between them like cloud shadow. Within it: the array-fenced paddocks; the king's riding court; the remount officers' hall.

SCALE: it is about 60 li (30 km) across, home to about 3,000,000 mortals, 2,500 at Qi Condensation, 100 at Foundation, 1 at Core Formation. Paint it at that true size from a nearby height: a great city in its own right that fills the whole view, streets as wide as rivers, its biggest halls the size of hills. Never a small medieval town.

The land: open grass plains to the horizon, wide sky, herds and wind.

The light: heavy rain at dusk, every street and roof a mirror. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Thousand Paddocks, Qianlan Cheng 千栏城, in the Border Wilds.

- The Spirit Herders' royal city, three million people inside a ring of array-fenced paddocks sixty li across, where the king holds court on horseback and the empire's remount officers keep a standing hall.

- Thousand Paddocks grew out of the first ranch, and the royal family still counts its wealth in heads. The paddocks are fenced with formation posts that keep a war mount in and a raiding beast out, and the court moves among them by season. Every great herder family keeps a hall here and sends its best riders to the king's games each autumn, where the empire's officers buy by the thousand. The Hundred Reins Sect's tamers bond the royal stud, and the Beast Court has twice sent killers into the palace paddocks.

- It is about 60 li (30 km) across.

- Its people: about 3,000,000 mortals, 2,500 at Qi Condensation, 100 at Foundation, 1 at Core Formation.

- Within it: the array-fenced paddocks; the king's riding court; the remount officers' hall.
```
