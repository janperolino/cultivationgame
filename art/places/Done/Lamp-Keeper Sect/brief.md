## 1. The banner: `place-shoudeng-zong-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Lamp-Keeper Sect (Shoudeng Zong 守灯宗), the seat of a sect, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Attached is Lamp-Keeper Sect as it is painted on the game's world map, seen from very high: this is the same place up close, so keep its architecture, its colours and the way it sits on the land.

Make it unmistakably Lamp-Keeper Sect, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game.

ITS LOOK: A ghost-way sect on a black fen: ten thousand pale-green lamps on poles in lines across black water, with the drowned circling beneath them, and a multi-roofed stilt hall at the centre lit warm amber among small pine islets. Mist, no roads, all of it on water. Colours: black water, pale lamp green, stilt-hall amber, mist grey-blue. It feels eerie, hushed, cold, patient. Leave out: streets, walls, red banners, dry ground, peaks, bright daylight, crowds.

From the world map it shows as: Ten thousand pale-green lamp points strung in lines across black fen water, and a stilt hall at their centre. Within it: lamp poles across the black water; the stilt hall in the fens; the drowned, circling under the lamps.

SCALE: it is about 40 li (20 km) across, home to about 2,000 mortals, 500 at Qi Condensation, 20 at Foundation. Paint it at that true size from a nearby height: a great city in its own right that fills the whole view, streets as wide as rivers, its biggest halls the size of hills. Never a small medieval town.

The land: black fen water, reeds, mist lying flat on the water, stilts and causeways.

The light: a storm rolling in, rain curtains and lightning far off. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Lamp-Keeper Sect, Shoudeng Zong 守灯宗, in the Border Wilds, a ghost-way sect.

- A ghost-way sect of five hundred that keeps the lamps of the Ghost-Lamp Fens lit, so the drowned stay in the water instead of walking to the City of the Guarded Mouth.

- The Ghost-Lamp Fens are named for the sect's lamps: ten thousand of them on poles across the water, burning yin fire that the drowned follow in circles instead of walking out. The City of the Guarded Mouth pays the sect to keep them lit, and has since the night a lamp line failed and the dead came to its walls. The Lamp-Keepers cultivate on the fens' yin qi, speak to the drowned, and sell what the drowned tell them.

- It is about 40 li (20 km) across.

- Its people: about 2,000 mortals, 500 at Qi Condensation, 20 at Foundation.

- Within it: lamp poles across the black water; the stilt hall in the fens; the drowned, circling under the lamps.
```
