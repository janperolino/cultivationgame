# Night Bridge Society 夜桥会, the art brief

The seat of a society, shown in the hall layout (A6). 3 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then 2 door sheets of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

- `place-yeqiao-hui-banner.png`
- `place-yeqiao-hui-doors-1.png`
- `place-yeqiao-hui-doors-2.png`

Run them in one chat, the banner first, so the door sheets keep its look.

## 1. The banner: `place-yeqiao-hui-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Night Bridge Society (Yeqiao Hui 夜桥会), the seat of a society, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Make it unmistakably Night Bridge Society, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game.

ITS LOOK: A smugglers’ chain of low reef islands: tide pools, wet black rock, sea caves with lantern-lit ledges, crude plank landings and rope bridges across the reef at low tide, a few crews’ lean-to huts, small flat boats pulled up on shingle. Nothing grand, everything hidden. Colours: wet black rock, tide-pool teal, dim lantern amber, night blue. It feels furtive, damp, nocturnal, tide-timed. Leave out: a city, grand halls, daylight crowds, walls, banners, soldiers.

From the world map it shows as: Nothing they would let you see: sea caves along the chain, and lantern lights moving island to island at low tide. Within it: a tide pool island on the chain; the low-tide reef between islands; a smugglers' sea cave.

The land: karst country: stone pillars by the hundred standing out of a sea of cloud, sinkholes and caves.

The light: late golden afternoon under heavy cloud, long shadows. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Night Bridge Society, Yeqiao Hui 夜桥会, in the Border Wilds, a neutral-way society.

- Half-blood smugglers who run the island chain in relays, one crew to a stretch, passing people, Gu eggs, beast cubs and herbs from island to island at low tide between the Morning Star Continent and Shared Roost City.

- The Society knows the tides, the reefs and which islands have something living on them this month. Most of its runners are Qi Condensation or Foundation half-bloods who know one stretch by heart, and each crew waits on its own tide, so a cargo takes about half a year to pass end to end; only the Core Formation leaders fly the whole chain, in about a month. It runs half-bloods out of human villages, beast eggs to the Kingdom of Spirit Herders's ranches, Gu to the marsh and Vast Wilds herbs to anyone. Its leaders are half-bloods with beast senses and long grudges. It pays tribute to nobody and has informers in every fort. Passage costs five low-grade stones, or a favor.

- Its people: 600 at Qi Condensation, 120 at Foundation, 4 at Core Formation.

- Within it: a tide pool island on the chain; the low-tide reef between islands; a smugglers' sea cave.
```
