# The Myriad Crafts Guild 万艺公会, the art brief

The seat of a guild, shown in the hall layout (A6). 2 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then one door sheet of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

## 1. The banner: `place-wanyi-hui-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of The Myriad Crafts Guild (Wanyi Gonghui 万艺公会), the seat of a guild, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

What it looks like: Nothing of its own: five chimneys smoking in a row behind Pill Street.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and drawn larger than life: tiered pagodas, palace halls with sweeping upturned golden eaves, pavilions on cliff edges, long carved stone stairs, jade bridges, walls as tall as cliffs, formation arrays as domes or rings of glowing runes, spirit-fire as pillars of light. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- The Myriad Crafts Guild, Wanyi Gonghui 万艺公会, in the Border Wilds, a neutral-way guild.

- The Morning Star Continent's guild of crafters, whose City of the Guarded Mouth branch tests the frontier's alchemists, refiners, talisman masters, array masters and spirit farmers and gives the badges a guild seat asks for.

- A crafter on the Morning Star Continent is asked two things before a price: which grade, and which guild hall sealed it. The Myriad Crafts Guild's City branch keeps five benches behind the Pill Street gate, one for each craft, and a crafter who brings their own inputs and makes one batch of a tier that holds, in front of its examiners, walks out with a badge. Its examiners are Foundation, so the frontier branch grades to second-grade and sends anyone better to the continent's halls. Its counter sells the frontier's common styles to anyone, and its members trade commissions and furnace time among themselves.

- It is about 1 li (1 km) across.

- Its people: about 800 mortals, 180 at Qi Condensation, 12 at Foundation.

- Within it: the five examination benches; the badge wall, every grade sealed in vermilion.
```

```text
Cells, left to right, top to bottom:

1. Buy in 入会: Where the way in is asked for, and the answer given at the gate.
2. Offer a gift: A gift laid before the steward is remembered as tribute.

4. The guild’s counter: The common styles of the area, sold openly to any crafter.
5. Your seat 席位: What the power wants of you this month, and what it gives.
6. Inside 内门: members only
7. Training ground 演武场: A drill ground where seniors spar with juniors, once a day each.
8. Elders’ hall 长老院: Where the elders sit: a lesson, a protector, a seat raised or a release.
9. Empty: plain dark mist.

People only small, in the middle distance, faceless; the place is the subject.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

The place, for mood: The Morning Star Continent's guild of crafters, whose City of the Guarded Mouth branch tests the frontier's alchemists, refiners, talisman masters, array masters and spirit farmers and gives the badges a guild seat asks for.
```
