# Night Bridge Society 夜桥会, the art brief

The seat of a society, shown in the hall layout (A6). 2 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then one door sheet of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

## 1. The banner: `place-yeqiao-hui-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Night Bridge Society (Yeqiao Hui 夜桥会), the seat of a society, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

What it looks like: Nothing they would let you see: sea caves along the chain, and lantern lights moving island to island at low tide.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and drawn larger than life: tiered pagodas, palace halls with sweeping upturned golden eaves, pavilions on cliff edges, long carved stone stairs, jade bridges, walls as tall as cliffs, formation arrays as domes or rings of glowing runes, spirit-fire as pillars of light. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Night Bridge Society, Yeqiao Hui 夜桥会, in the Border Wilds, a neutral-way society.

- Half-blood smugglers who run the island chain in relays, one crew to a stretch, passing people, Gu eggs, beast cubs and herbs from island to island at low tide between the Morning Star Continent and Shared Roost City.

- The Society knows the tides, the reefs and which islands have something living on them this month. Most of its runners are Qi Condensation or Foundation half-bloods who know one stretch by heart, and each crew waits on its own tide, so a cargo takes about half a year to pass end to end; only the Core Formation leaders fly the whole chain, in about a month. It runs half-bloods out of human villages, beast eggs to the Kingdom of Spirit Herders's ranches, Gu to the marsh and Vast Wilds herbs to anyone. Its leaders are half-bloods with beast senses and long grudges. It pays tribute to nobody and has informers in every fort. Passage costs five low-grade stones, or a favor.

- Its people: 600 at Qi Condensation, 120 at Foundation, 4 at Core Formation.

- Within it: a tide pool island on the chain; the low-tide reef between islands; a smugglers' sea cave.
```

All nine are views inside Night Bridge Society (夜桥会), the seat of a society: the same place, the same light and palette in every cell, each scene's subject in its upper two thirds (the card's name covers the bottom third).

Cells, left to right, top to bottom:

1. Ask to be sponsored 引荐: Where the way in is asked for, and the answer given at the gate.
2. Offer a gift: A gift laid before the steward is remembered as tribute.

5. Scripture pavilion 藏经阁: The sect’s own chapters, on shelves that open by seat.

7. Inside 内门: members only
8. Training ground 演武场: A drill ground where seniors spar with juniors, once a day each.
9. Elders’ hall 长老院: Where the elders sit: a lesson, a protector, a seat raised or a release.

People only small, in the middle distance, faceless; the place is the subject.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

The place, for mood: Half-blood smugglers who run the island chain in relays, one crew to a stretch, passing people, Gu eggs, beast cubs and herbs from island to island at low tide between the Morning Star Continent and Shared Roost City.
