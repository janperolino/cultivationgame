## 1. The banner: `place-hexue-jing-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Realm of Mingled Blood (Hexue Jing 合血境), a sect behind its gate and array, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Make it unmistakably Realm of Mingled Blood, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game. What sets it apart: Nothing while it sleeps, its gate a cave mouth in the karst; for the thirty days it opens, a column of green light stands over the cave. Within it: the gate cave in the karst; a ruined village of the one people; a bloodline altar; herb meadows under a green sky.

The land: old forest, giant trees, roots and moss, light falling in shafts.

The light: a red sunset smouldering through smoke. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Realm of Mingled Blood, Hexue Jing 合血境, in the Border Wilds.

- A shard world whose gate opens in a karst cave every seven years. It admits only those below Foundation who carry beast blood, or a tamer bonded to a beast.

- Inside is a green world where beasts and humans once lived as one people, with ruined villages, bloodline altars and herbs that awaken dormant bloodlines. Half-bloods go in to learn what they are; tamers go in to strengthen their bonds; pure humans who try the gate are thrown back with broken bones. The Night Bridge Society guards the cave and charges entry. It last opened five years ago.

- Within it: the gate cave in the karst; a ruined village of the one people; a bloodline altar; herb meadows under a green sky.
```
