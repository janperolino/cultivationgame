## 1. The banner: `place-xingyun-yizhi-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Ruins of the Starfall Sect (Xingyun Zong Yizhi 星陨宗遗址), a landmark, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Make it unmistakably Ruins of the Starfall Sect, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game. What sets it apart: A broken mountain under black pine forest, its pieces scattered across a hundred and fifty li, a crater at its heart that faintly glows. Within it: broken halls under blackpines; star-iron in the rubble; the glowing ground at the heart.

The land: old forest, giant trees, roots and moss, light falling in shafts.

The light: a red sunset smouldering through smoke. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Ruins of the Starfall Sect, Xingyun Zong Yizhi 星陨宗遗址, in the Border Wilds.

- The ruins of a sect that ruled the Border Wilds eight hundred years ago until a beast tide broke its mountain, sunk in the Blackpine Forest. Its halls are rubble; what it kept underneath is not.

- The Starfall Sect drew its arts from a fallen star and held the frontier for three thousand years. Eight hundred years ago a beast tide led by a king of the Vast Wilds broke its mountain in a night, and the forest has grown over the pieces. Scavengers still find star-iron in the rubble. Every few decades the ground at the heart of the ruins glows, and those who go in when it does come out changed, or not at all.

- It is about 150 li (75 km) across.

- Within it: broken halls under blackpines; star-iron in the rubble; the glowing ground at the heart.
```
