# Low-Tide Tower 落潮楼, the art brief

The seat of a assassins, shown in the hall layout (A6). 2 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then one door sheet of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

## 1. The banner: `place-luochao-lou-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Low-Tide Tower (Luochao Lou 落潮楼), the seat of a assassins, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

What it looks like: Nothing: open sea over a reef, and at the lowest tide the top of a drowned tower.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and drawn larger than life: tiered pagodas, palace halls with sweeping upturned golden eaves, pavilions on cliff edges, long carved stone stairs, jade bridges, walls as tall as cliffs, formation arrays as domes or rings of glowing runes, spirit-fire as pillars of light. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Low-Tide Tower, Luochao Lou 落潮楼, in the Border Wilds, a demonic-way assassins.

- An assassins’ house hidden inside the Night Bridge Society, whose killers cross the strait in the Society’s relays at low tide and are gone again before the tide turns.

- The Low-Tide Tower is an assassins’ house of perhaps a hundred killers who cross the strait with the Night Bridge crews and come back with them, so a killing on the chain or in the City is half a year cold before anyone looks for who did it. A contract is left at the Nameless Market as a name and a price in a sealed jade slip; the Tower takes it or the slip comes back blank. Its killers are graded bronze, silver and gold by the contracts they have closed, and none of them knows more than one runner of the Society by face. It keeps no hall above the water: its house is a drowned watchtower that stands clear of the sea for an hour at the lowest tide.

- Its people: 80 at Qi Condensation, 15 at Foundation, 1 at Core Formation.

- Within it: a drowned watchtower that stands clear of the sea only at the lowest tide; the contract wall, names scratched into wet stone; the reef path out, gone when the tide turns.
```
