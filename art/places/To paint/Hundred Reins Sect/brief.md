# Hundred Reins Sect 百缰宗, the art brief

The seat of a sect, shown in the hall layout (A6). 3 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then 2 door sheets of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

## 1. The banner: `place-baijiang-zong-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Hundred Reins Sect (Baijiang Zong 百缰宗), the seat of a sect, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

What it looks like: A sect spread across rolling grass in rings of stables and sparring fields, storm-hawks circling it in a slow wheel.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and drawn larger than life: tiered pagodas, palace halls with sweeping upturned golden eaves, pavilions on cliff edges, long carved stone stairs, jade bridges, walls as tall as cliffs, formation arrays as domes or rings of glowing runes, spirit-fire as pillars of light. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Hundred Reins Sect, Baijiang Zong 百缰宗, in the Border Wilds, a orthodox-way sect.

- A beast-taming sect of three thousand that the Spirit Herders back, teaching the bond between rider and beast as a Dao. It is in a blood feud with the Beast-Butcher Gate, who call it the collaborators' sect.

- The Hundred Reins Sect bonds its disciples to beasts the way the Herders bond their children, but as cultivation: a disciple and a beast cultivate as one, and the strongest pairs fight as a single cultivator a realm above either. It trains the royal stud at Thousand Paddocks and the Herders pay its keep. The Beast-Butcher Gate kills tamed beasts on sight as Beast Court spies, the Reins kill butchers who do it, and the feud has run a hundred and twenty years. Its elders want a thunder-ape cub from Ape-Cry Peaks, and three teams have died trying.

- It is about 150 li (75 km) across.

- Its people: about 20,000 mortals, 3,000 at Qi Condensation, 120 at Foundation, 3 at Core Formation.

- Within it: the bonding stables; the open grass where pairs spar; the hatchery kept warm by array.
```
