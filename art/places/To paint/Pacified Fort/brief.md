# Pacified Fort 安边堡, the art brief

A town, the seat of a county, shown in the grid layout (A6). 3 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then 2 door sheets of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

## 1. The banner: `place-pacified-fort-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Pacified Fort (Anbian Bao 安边堡), a town, the seat of a county, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

What it looks like: A war city on a hill crown cut flat, ringed by walls of its own rock as tall as cliffs, its drill grounds pale scars across the plateau, a pale lockdown dome over it, and the March’s beacon as a pillar of spirit-fire on its highest bastion.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and drawn larger than life: tiered pagodas, palace halls with sweeping upturned golden eaves, pavilions on cliff edges, long carved stone stairs, jade bridges, walls as tall as cliffs, formation arrays as domes or rings of glowing runes, spirit-fire as pillars of light. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Pacified Fort, Anbian Bao 安边堡, in the Border Wilds.

- The Gongsun war city and the March’s seat, cut into the crown of a hill the first general flattened with one stroke. Its walls are the hill’s own rock, raised by formation to the height of cliffs; inside them, barracks, armouries and drill grounds hold four hundred thousand under arms, and every sixteen-year-old the March owes the garrison passes through its cadet school. The March’s beacon burns on its highest bastion, and a lockdown array rises over it as a dome of pale light when beasts come.

- It is about 40 li (20 km) across.

- Its people: about 1,500,000 mortals, 1,500 at Qi Condensation, 120 at Foundation.

- Within it: the beacon bastion; the drill ground on the cut plateau; the armouries under the walls; the cadet school’s lecture halls.
```
