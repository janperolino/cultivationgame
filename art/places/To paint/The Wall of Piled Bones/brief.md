# The Wall of Piled Bones 堆骨墙, the art brief

A landmark, shown in the hall layout (A6). 2 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then one door sheet of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

## 1. The banner: `place-duigu-qiang-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of The Wall of Piled Bones (Duigu Qiang 堆骨墙), a landmark, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

What it looks like: A bone-white rampart as tall as cliffs running 9,000 li over the coastal hills, beast-king skulls the size of hills for its gatehouses and a thread of binding-array light along its top.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and drawn larger than life: tiered pagodas, palace halls with sweeping upturned golden eaves, pavilions on cliff edges, long carved stone stairs, jade bridges, walls as tall as cliffs, formation arrays as domes or rings of glowing runes, spirit-fire as pillars of light. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- The Wall of Piled Bones, Duigu Qiang 堆骨墙, in the Border Wilds.

- A wall of rammed earth and beast-king bone 9,000 li long along the coastal hills, as tall as cliffs and built over a thousand years of raids. On Ghost Month nights the bones howl.

- Each garrison builds in the bones of the beasts it kills, and the oldest stretches are faced with the skulls of beast kings, each the size of a hill, their eye sockets now the wall's gates. Binding arrays run along the top as a thread of light and hold the beast spirits down, all but the seventh month, when they haunt it and ghost-hunters and bone-collectors come. Old bones from the wall are a refining material for beast-taming whistles and Slaughter Dao artifacts, and stealing them is a hanging offense. The March of Pacified Borders garrisons the southern stretch, and its cadets learn the wall by patrolling it.

- It is about 9,000 li (4,500 km) across.

- Its people: about 200,000 mortals, 1,500 at Qi Condensation, 80 at Foundation, 2 at Core Formation.

- Within it: the patrol walk along the top; the old stretches faced with beast-king skulls, their eye sockets gates; bone heaps waiting to be built in.
```
