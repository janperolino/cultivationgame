# Pavilion of Bound Bones 缚骨阁, the art brief

The seat of a sect, shown in the hall layout (A6). 3 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then 2 door sheets of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

- `place-fugu-ge-banner.png`
- `place-fugu-ge-doors-1.png`
- `place-fugu-ge-doors-2.png`

Run them in one chat, the banner first, so the door sheets keep its look.

## 1. The banner: `place-fugu-ge-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Pavilion of Bound Bones (Fugu Ge 缚骨阁), the seat of a sect, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Make it unmistakably Pavilion of Bound Bones, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game. What sets it apart: A hall standing in the eye socket of a beast-king skull the size of a hill in the Wall, with the binding array's thread of light running away from it along the Wall in both directions. Within it: the hall in the great skull's eye socket; the array plates along the Wall's top; the bone-reading room.

The land: folded green hill country, terraced fields in the valleys, beacon peaks on the ridgelines.

The light: a red sunset smouldering through smoke. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Pavilion of Bound Bones, Fugu Ge 缚骨阁, in the Border Wilds, an orthodox-way sect.

- An array sect of two and a half thousand that the empire pays to keep the Wall of Piled Bones' binding arrays lit, so the beast spirits in its bones stay down all but one month a year.

- The Pavilion of Bound Bones was founded to bind the Wall's dead, and the empire's stipend has kept it doing nothing else for six hundred years. Its disciples walk the Wall's 9,000 li in relays, renewing the array along the top, and its hall stands in the eye socket of the greatest skull in the Wall. In Ghost Month the arrays are let down on purpose, because holding them all year cracks the bones, and the Pavilion spends the month with the Temple of the Quiet Bell quieting what comes up. It wants the Black Jade Vein's jade for its array plates, and so does the Beast-Butcher Gate.

- It is about 120 li (60 km) across.

- Its people: about 15,000 mortals, 2,500 at Qi Condensation, 120 at Foundation, 3 at Core Formation.

- Within it: the hall in the great skull's eye socket; the array plates along the Wall's top; the bone-reading room.
```
