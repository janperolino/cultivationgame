# Kingdom of Spirit Herders 牧灵国, the art brief

The hall of the Kingdom of Spirit Herders at Thousand Paddocks, shown in the hall layout (A6). 2 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then one door sheet of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

## 1. The banner: `place-muling-guo-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Kingdom of Spirit Herders (Muling Guo 牧灵国), the hall of the Kingdom of Spirit Herders at Thousand Paddocks, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

What it looks like: the Kingdom of Spirit Herders's own hall inside Thousand Paddocks: Open grass to every edge, the herds' trails worn into it like braided rivers, and the ranch steadings as pale rings of array-fenced pasture.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and drawn larger than life: tiered pagodas, palace halls with sweeping upturned golden eaves, pavilions on cliff edges, long carved stone stairs, jade bridges, walls as tall as cliffs, formation arrays as domes or rings of glowing runes, spirit-fire as pillars of light. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Kingdom of Spirit Herders, Muling Guo 牧灵国, in the Border Wilds.

- A kingdom of beast tamers on 9,000 li of grassland, who raise war mounts for the empire by the hundred thousand from eggs smuggled out of the Vast Wilds Continent. The Beast Court has put a price on every one of them.

- The Kingdom of Spirit Herders's ranches raise thunder-horned deer, scale-horses and war hounds from eggs and cubs bought from smugglers, in herds that take days to ride past. The empire buys every mount at a good price, and the herder families are rich, armed and hunted. Beast Court agents, half-bloods in its pay and now and then an old beast, cross the strait to kill tamers and free their beasts. Children are bonded to a hatchling at seven and grow up with it.

- It is about 9,000 li (4,500 km) across.

- Its people: about 32,000,000 mortals, 10,000 at Qi Condensation, 200 at Foundation.

- Within it: ranch corrals of thunder-horned deer and scale-horses; open grass to the horizon; the hatchling barns.
```
