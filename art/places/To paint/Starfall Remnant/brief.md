## 1. The banner: `place-xingyun-jing-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Starfall Remnant (Xingyun Canjing 星陨残境), a sect behind its gate and array, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Make it unmistakably Starfall Remnant, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game. What sets it apart: Nothing while it sleeps; for its ten days, a column of starlight over the ruins' heart. Within it: the halls as they were the night the mountain fell; the star in its vault; the guardians the elders left.

The land: old forest, giant trees, roots and moss, light falling in shafts.

The light: a bright overcast morning, soft light, colours clear. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Starfall Remnant, Xingyun Canjing 星陨残境, in the Border Wilds.

- The Starfall Sect's last hall, folded into a pocket of space under its ruins when the mountain broke. It opens for ten days every thirty years, when the ground at the ruins' heart glows.

- When the beast tide broke the mountain, the Starfall Sect's last elders folded their library and their star into a pocket of space and sealed themselves in with it. Every thirty years the seal thins, and cultivators below Core Formation can slip through. Inside are the sect's halls as they were, the arts it drew from its star, and the guardians it left.

- Within it: the halls as they were the night the mountain fell; the star in its vault; the guardians the elders left.
```
