## 1. The banner: `place-anbian-fu-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of March of Pacified Borders (Anbian Fu 安边府), the hall of the March of Pacified Borders at River-Bend City, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

What it looks like: the March of Pacified Borders's own hall inside River-Bend City: Folded hill country with its counties' fields in the valleys, the beacon peaks' pillars of spirit-fire along its borders, and the pale dome over Pacified Fort.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and drawn larger than life: tiered pagodas, palace halls with sweeping upturned golden eaves, pavilions on cliff edges, long carved stone stairs, jade bridges, walls as tall as cliffs, formation arrays as domes or rings of glowing runes, spirit-fire as pillars of light. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- March of Pacified Borders, Anbian Fu 安边府, in the Border Wilds.

- The empire's frontier march, 12,000 li of hill country ruled by the Gongsun family of generals: some forty million farmers who plow with spears at hand, and a line of beacon peaks whose spirit-fire carries a raid warning across the march in a night.

- The Gongsuns have held the march for nine generations and buried most of their sons on the wall. Every family owes one child to the garrison at sixteen, which keeps a million and a half under arms across the march, the Wall of Piled Bones and the coast. Rooted children are another matter: since the raids tripled, the Gongsuns hold a frontier charter, so the Registry's frontier tester comes at twelve, the age the march can put a child on the wall, and the garrison keeps every rooted child for its cadet school instead of sending them to the capital. Its best leave all the same, for the City of the Guarded Mouth or the Beast-Butcher Gate, so the march itself has no one past Foundation. Children here learn to read beast tracks before letters and to tell a raid horn from a hunt horn. A beast core turned in at a fort earns a bounty in rice or stones.

- It is about 12,000 li (6,000 km) across.

- Its people: about 44,000,000 mortals, 12,000 at Qi Condensation, 200 at Foundation.

- Within it: a village watchtower; a beast-pit; fields plowed with spears at hand.
```
