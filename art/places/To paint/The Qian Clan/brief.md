# The Qian Clan 钱氏, the art brief

The seat of a clan, shown in the hall layout (A6). 2 ChatGPT runs: the banner, then one door sheet of nine. Save each result into `art/inbox/` under its name below, run `npm run art`, and move this folder to `Done/` once all are in.

- `place-qian-shi-banner.png`
- `place-qian-shi-doors-1.png`

## 1. The banner: `place-qian-shi-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of The Qian Clan (Qian Shi 钱氏), the seat of a clan, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

What it looks like: Nothing of its own: a gilded roof among the City's millions.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail and calm, restrained light, in a deep, dark-toned palette (dusk, lamplight, mist, the night sky) so that gold and ivory text reads over it, with a little muted cyan qi light where cultivation shows; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and drawn larger than life: tiered pagodas, palace halls with sweeping upturned golden eaves, pavilions on cliff edges, long carved stone stairs, jade bridges, walls as tall as cliffs, formation arrays as domes or rings of glowing runes, spirit-fire as pillars of light. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- The Qian Clan, Qian Shi 钱氏, in the Border Wilds, a neutral-way clan.

- A cultivating merchant clan of the City of the Guarded Mouth that sits between the bounty office, the Beast-Butcher Gate and Apricot Grove Valley's buyers, and takes a cut of every beast core that leaves the Border Wilds.

- The Qians came to the frontier four generations ago with one shop and a contract for Gate cores, and now hold the City's core exchange. Their agents buy at River-Bend and the forts before cores reach the bounty office, grade them, and sell the best to Apricot Grove Valley's frontier hall. They are polite to everyone and owe nobody, and a cultivator short of stones can always sell them a future.

- It is about 2 li (1 km) across.

- Its people: about 5,000 mortals, 200 at Qi Condensation, 12 at Foundation.

- Within it: the core exchange, graded by lamp; the clan vault under the exchange.
```
