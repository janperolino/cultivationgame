## 1. The banner: `place-duanchao-yuan-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Field of the Broken Tide (Duanchao Yuan 断潮原), a landmark, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Make it unmistakably Field of the Broken Tide, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game. What sets it apart: White salt flats on the coast scattered with the bones of beasts the size of hills, and on still nights a faint grey shimmer of ranks drawn up across them. Within it: salt flats white with bone; the ghost armies forming up on still nights; artifacts in the salt.

The land: a sea coast: surf, reefs, sea cliffs, harbour water and salt haze.

The light: a storm rolling in, rain curtains and lightning far off. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Field of the Broken Tide, Duanchao Yuan 断潮原, in the Border Wilds.

- Salt flats three hundred li across on the southern coast where the empire first held the shore against a beast tide a thousand years ago. The tide's dead and the empire's still stand where they fell.

- A thousand years ago a beast tide came over the chain and the empire met it on the flats with forty thousand cultivators and a million soldiers. The line held and the flats have been white with salt and bone ever since. On still nights the armies form up again, and the Temple of the Quiet Bell keeps them from marching. Scavengers dig old artifacts out of the salt; most of them come back.

- It is about 300 li (150 km) across.

- Within it: salt flats white with bone; the ghost armies forming up on still nights; artifacts in the salt.
```
