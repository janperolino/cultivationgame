## 1. The banner: `place-heiyu-mai-banner.png`

```text
Create ONE landscape 1536x1024 painting AS AN ASSET, placed behind real HTML text and buttons on a game screen: the banner of Black Jade Vein (Heiyu Mai 黑玉脉), a landmark, as a traveller arriving sees it from a height nearby.

Make it unmistakably Black Jade Vein, unlike any other city, town or sect in the game. What sets it apart: Gallery mouths and spoil heaps scarring the foot of the needle peaks, a dark seam glinting where the jade breaks the surface. Within it: galleries cut through black jade; the contested middle shafts; spoil heaps at the gallery mouths.

The land: karst country: stone pillars by the hundred standing out of a sea of cloud, sinkholes and caves.

The light: heavy rain at dusk, every street and roof a mirror. Keep the whole on the dark side, so gold and ivory text reads over it.

The game shows a wide strip of it, from about 20% to 60% of the height, under its name and lore: put the place itself in that band and in the right two thirds; keep the left third calmer and darker (the text sits over it); above the band quiet sky, below it the near ground fading to dark.

Paint the place only: no people close up, no cultivator, no creatures unless named here.

STYLE: Chinese xianxia cultivation fantasy, as in Chinese cultivation games and donghua, painted with rich detail; never medieval European, never sci-fi. The world is vast, at continental scale: a city is a sea of roofs to the horizon, a village is the seat of a county the size of a country. What cultivators build is grand and larger than life, but paint only what this place has, never a stock xianxia city. Rock stays natural rock. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO UI, no signs with writing, no frame, no watermark.

About the place, for its look and mood (none of this is written on the image):

- Black Jade Vein, Heiyu Mai 黑玉脉, in the Border Wilds.

- A spirit-jade vein under the southern Ape-Cry Peaks, the richest in the Border Wilds, mined in galleries the Pavilion of Bound Bones and the Beast-Butcher Gate each claim, while the thunder-apes bring the mountain down on both.

- Black jade holds an array's charge better than any stone on the frontier and takes a saber's edge like steel, so two sects want all of it. The Pavilion's miners hold the upper galleries and the Gate's the lower, and the shafts between are where they fight. Above both, the thunder-apes drop rock on anyone they hear digging.

- It is about 400 li (200 km) across.

- Within it: galleries cut through black jade; the contested middle shafts; spoil heaps at the gallery mouths.
```
