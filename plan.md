# Build plan

The game is built in phases, and each phase is sized for one Claude Code session. A new session starts clean, reads this file, does one phase, records what it did, and ends. Nothing has to be carried from one session to the next except what is written here, in the code and in the tests.

The rules live in `docs/design.md` and the world in `content/atlas/entries.json`. Both local files are the source of truth; the claude.ai design doc and atlas they came from are frozen archives since 2026-10-11.

## How each session runs

1. Open Claude Code in this folder and say: **"Do phase P0.1 from plan.md."** (Use the next unticked phase.)
2. Claude reads `CLAUDE.md` (automatic), this file's Status, Open questions and that phase, then only the `docs/design.md` sections the phase lists. It searches by heading rather than reading the whole file.
   - **The phase talk** (Jan's call, 2026-10-14): before building, the session talks the phase through with Jan to flesh it out, starting from its "Talk through first" list when it has one. Design sessions settle the shape and the key calls; the details wait for this talk.
   - **What might change it later** (Jan's call, 2026-10-17): an A phase, and any big style change or overhaul, also goes over in its talk what later milestones and phases might change about it (the screens they add or fold, the systems that will crowd them, the concepts they'll want), so its look leaves room for them rather than being redone.
3. If the phase has a **Before the session** line, Jan does that first. The session checks it and stops if it isn't done.
4. It stays inside the phase. If something comes up that the design doesn't answer, it picks the simplest option that fits, writes it under Open questions and carries on. It stops to ask only when the answer would change the design. It adds whatever npm packages the phase needs.
5. Content a session drafts (talents, tree nodes) is committed as data and listed in the Log as awaiting review. Jan reviews it after the session and leaves notes under Open questions, and the next session applies them before its own phase.
6. The phase is finished when its "Done when" list holds and `npm test`, `npm run lint` and `npm run build` pass.
7. Before ending, it ticks the phase in Status, adds 3–5 lines to the Log (what was built, decisions made, what the next phase should know) and commits as `P0.1: <summary>`.
8. Close the session. The next phase starts in a fresh one.

**Design sessions** (D2, D3 and so on) run here like any phase: they edit `docs/design.md` and the atlas directly, then tick, log and commit. `node tools/atlas-serve.cjs` opens the atlas map for browsing and editing.

**Planning sessions** happen between phases whenever Jan wants one. They discuss the design and edit `docs/design.md`, `plan.md` and the atlas, never code. Each one adds a Log line and commits on its own as `design: <summary>`, separate from any phase commit. Sessions may run in parallel, each in its own worktree on its own branch (Jan's call, 2026-10-18): a worktree session edits only its own phase's lines in this file (its Status tick, its spec, its Log entry), so merges stay clean, and the design sessions, which all edit `docs/design.md`, run one at a time on main.

**At each milestone's close**, the planning session that opens the next design session compacts this file, since every session reads Status and Open questions: the milestone's phase specs, its Log entries and the Open questions it settled move verbatim to `docs/plan-archive.md`, after any rule that lived only here is written into `docs/design.md`. Open questions keeps what is open, what awaits Jan's review, and what the running milestone settled that its next phases still read; the Log keeps the running milestone in full and one line a phase for the rest.

## The layers

Built bottom-up since 2026-10-18 (Jan's call): what other systems read is built first, so a later system plugs into it rather than rebuilding it. Each layer is thin, the least the layers after it need, and opens with its design session, which expands it into phases in Status, and ends with a play session on the dev console. The deep versions come back once there is something real to tune them against. Each bullet says what the layer reads and what reads it; the outline it draws from is below.

- **The economy, thin** (design § Economy; § World bible, The world at its true size and How many live there). Reads A5.2's veins, population and prosperity and P3.5's shops and catalogs. Read by organizations (contribution, sect shops), jobs (pay), crafts (what ingredients cost and what sells) and trade. From M6: veins as where the money comes from (the stone grade a vein yields, A5.2's Log), prosperity drifting within the vein and driving what a place makes and needs, shops' stock and money by it, prices that move with supply, and markets by reach. Its design session took D6's economy half and your own trade (2026-10-19, Jan's call: what reads only markets is built here, what reads organizations, jobs or crafts waits); its phases are below.
- **Organizations, thin** (design § Professions, people and powers). Reads P3.4's people and standing, P3.4b's thin membership and the economy. Read by jobs (a job is a post), crafts (alchemy shops and refiners as a power's), trade (the merchant guilds) and the AI half's scenes. From M5: one skeleton for sects, clans, guilds and factions, the atlas's powers tagged by type, ranks, posts and contribution, and P3.5's sect shops priced in contribution. Its design session took D5's organization half and folded in P3.7b's jobs as O4 (2026-10-19, Jan's call); its phases are below.
- **Crafts: M4** (the outline below; D4 first). Reads the economy and organizations. Read by trade (what moves between markets) and the AI half (crafting in scenes).
- **The atlas fill: the other continents** (`content/atlas/README.md`; A5.2 filled the Morning Star Continent, its 133 entries the pattern, with How things connect in hand and cheaper agents a region at a time). Read by trade's long travel. It can run beside the layers above, one session on `entries.json` at a time.
- **Guild trade and the living world** (the rest of M5 and M6 below; a design session first). Reads every layer above. Your own trade is the economy's; this is guilds trading from region to region (moving the economy's supplies from market to market), caravans, commissions, the black market, auctions, the events calendar, NPCs who age and climb, rumors and headlines, map tiers and long travel.
